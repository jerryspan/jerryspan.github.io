⚖️ [JUST-IN](https://cordis.europa.eu/project/id/101289801) kick-off meeting on October 8th/9th. UM's role will be to design the Embedded Judicial Independence Tracker (EJIT) 📈, an online platform that brings together comparative data on judicial independence across nine jurisdictions 🗺️ and the AI-assisted cross-citation support tool 🤖, which helps judges and lawyers find relevant case law from other countries.
