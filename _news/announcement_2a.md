🏳️‍🌈📊 WinoQueer-NL paper has been accepted at [GebNLP 2026](https://gebnlp-workshop.github.io/)! This paper brings the first Dutch-language benchmark (based on a community survey) for measuring anti-queer bias in AI language models (work led by master student Jiska Beuk). Pre-print [here](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.02651), data [here](https://github.com/jerryspan/WinoQueer-NL)
